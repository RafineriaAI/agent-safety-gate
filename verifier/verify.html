agent-safety-gate — offline record verifier

Drop a decision record file here. This page recomputes every digest, follows the hash chain and checks every Ed25519 signature, entirely inside your browser. Nothing is uploaded, and nothing can be: the page declares a content security policy that forbids network access.

Drop a .jsonl record file here or click to choose one — yours or ours

What a green result means. The file was not altered after it was written, each record links to the one before it, and each record was signed by the key it names. A signature is unforgeable without that private key. It is not identity assurance on its own: paste the key you expect into the field above to turn “signed by some key” into “signed by the key I expect”.

What it does not mean. Nothing here judges whether an action was a good idea. The verdict in a record is the gate’s deterministic reading of the signals it was given under the policy named in that record.
